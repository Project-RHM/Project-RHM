 1. Find an antiderivative of [`\cos\left(x\right) - 4 \cdot x^{3}`] with respect to x, with C = 0.
    answer: Formula(sin(x)-x^4)
 2. Find an antiderivative of [`\cos\left(x\right) - 4 \cdot 2 \cdot x`] with respect to x, with C = 0.
    answer: Formula(sin(x)-4*x^2)
 3. Find an antiderivative of [`\cos\left(x\right) - 4 \cdot 2 \cdot x`] with respect to x, with C = 0.
    answer: Formula(sin(x)-4*x^2)
 4. Find an antiderivative of [`\cos\left(x\right) - 4 \cdot 4 \cdot x^{3}`] with respect to x, with C = 0.
    answer: Formula(sin(x)-4*x^4)
 5. Find an antiderivative of [`3 \cdot 3 \cdot x^{2} + \cos\left(x\right)`] with respect to x, with C = 0.
